About this data
Status tags (Cabinet wheels). Verified on a current official page, a 2026 government record, or a GEDS entry updated in 2026. Announced named by the Prime Minister but contradicted by an older record. Re-check newest source predates 2026. Not named no source names this person.
Shades of grey group portfolios by theme to make each wheel readable. They are an editorial grouping, not an official structure, and they don't represent political parties.
Federal ministers come from the Prime Minister's Cabinet page and deputy heads were cross-checked against the GEDS export of Oct 25, 2026. BC data comes from the Province's Executive Council and Deputy Ministers' Council pages. Victoria data comes from the 2026 preliminary election results, the City's audited 2024 and 2025 Annual Reports and the 2025–2029 and 2026–2030 Financial Plans; the Victoria scorecard's status labels compare the City's Strategic Plan with its own Annual Reports. This is a snapshot; confirm anything important at the source linked in each profile.
